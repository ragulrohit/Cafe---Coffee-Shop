Our Story

From One Roaster To Forty Tables

In 2017 our founder, Aarav Mehta, came home from a decade of coffee work in Kyoto and Bengaluru with one clear idea: the city deserved a roastery that roasted on site, calibrated honestly and never rushed a single cup. He leased a 40 sqm unit on Roastery Lane, bought a used 12 kg drum roaster and opened at seven in the morning with twelve regulars.

Eight years later STACKLY runs three counters, a bakery kitchen and a training academy for new baristas. The chairs are still mismatched. The obsession is exactly the same.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Philosophy

Three sentences we can all recite without looking them up.

Our Mission

To make a genuinely excellent cup of coffee something anyone can walk into a cafe and order — without jargon, without pretension, and without a compromise on the bean.

Our Vision

A city of cafes where the barista knows your order, the farmer is named on the menu, and roasting happens within walking distance of the counter that serves it.

Our Philosophy

Coffee is agriculture first, craft second. We roast to reveal the harvest rather than to impose a house style, and we adjust the recipe to the bean — never the other way round.

“If you cannot explain what a lot tastes like, you should not be pouring it. Our job is to hand you the harvest, not our personality.”

How We Got Here

Milestones On The Long Way Round

Not a straight line — but every step mattered.

2017

One Roaster, Three Chairs

Aarav opens a 40 sqm unit on Roastery Lane with a second-hand drum roaster and a promise: roast on site, every week, no shortcuts.

2019

The Academy Opens

Baristas keep asking us how we train them, so we turn it into a six-week free course. Thirty-one graduates later, it is still running.

2021

Direct Trade, Eleven Farms

We sign first direct contracts with eleven farms, pay 40% above the Fairtrade floor and publish every lot's price on the menu board.

2023

Zero Plastic, Full Kitchen

We retire the last of the single-use plastic and open an in-house bakery, so the croissant you eat was proofed four doors away.

2026

Three Counters & A Dashboard

Our own roasting and cafe operations now run on the STACKLY dashboard — same transparency we promise guests, applied to ourselves.
