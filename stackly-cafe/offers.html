How To Redeem

Three steps. If a barista says otherwise, they are wrong and this page is the referee.

1. Claim It Here

Tap the claim button on the offer you want. It marks itself claimed and confirms with a message — no email required.

2. Name The Code

Say the code printed on the offer card. For offers with no code, name the offer instead. That is the whole process.

3. Log In To Stack Points

Log in before you order and every rupee counts toward your tier automatically. Points post the same evening.

4. Enjoy Your Reward

Show your claimed offer at the counter, enjoy your coffee and keep collecting points on every visit.

Can I stack two offers?

One offer per order. Tier discounts are separate — an Insider saves their 10% on whatever remains after the offer applies.

Do offers apply to delivery?

They apply at every STACKLY counter and in the app. Third-party delivery platforms carry their own fees and cannot honour codes.

Any offers for large orders?

Ten cups or more, email us and we will build a custom brew box with the blend and a price that makes sense for the office.

Something still not clear?

Call the counter on +91 98765 43210 or send the team a note from the contact page. A human replies within 24 hours.
