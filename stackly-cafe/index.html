The STACKLY Difference

Why Choose STACKLY

Four reasons regulars keep coming back — and keep bringing friends.

Freshly Roasted

Our 12 kg drum roaster runs four days a week. Beans leave the roaster within 48 hours, so your cup tastes of the harvest, not the shelf.

Premium Ingredients

100% Arabica, cold-pressed milk, real cocoa and single-origin chocolate. Nothing artificial, nothing shy about real ingredients.

Expert Baristas

15 trained baristas, every one of them certified in espresso calibration and milk chemistry. Ask them anything — they love a question.

Cozy Atmosphere

Warm timber, brass fittings, soft jazz and 40 seats with plug points. Built for a two-hour read or a two-minute catch-up.
